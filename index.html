MoodShift Analytics collects the channel owner’s YouTube analytics on their own computer, supporting content planning for ambient and nature soundscapes.

Owner-only · Local collection · Read-only access
01 / CONNECT

Authorize with Google

The owner signs in through Google and grants permission to read their channel metadata and analytics. The tool does not receive or store a Google password.

02 / COLLECT

Keep the history locally

A local collector retrieves daily reports and video metadata. An optional Windows scheduled task runs collection while the owner’s computer is available.

03 / REVIEW

Plan the next experiment

CSV files and editable creative labels help the owner compare video themes. A separate export can be created and shared manually for analysis.

Purpose & access

Built for one channel owner

MoodShift Analytics is a personal desktop workflow, not a public analytics service. It reads video details and aggregate performance measures such as views, watch time, thumbnail impressions, click-through rate, and traffic sources.

It creates reporting jobs and downloads the resulting data. It does not upload videos or change titles, thumbnails, playlists, comments, or channel permissions.

This website provides information about the tool. There is no hosted sign-in, file upload, or analytics dashboard on this site.
